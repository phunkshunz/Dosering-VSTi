DOSERING

ONE KNOB LOW END · DUCK · SUB · TEAR

DOSERING doses the low end. One knob makes the sound duck in time, fills the gap with a sub that starts in phase when the kick lets go, and finally tears the bottom up with saturation. No sidechain routing needed.

VST3 · AU · LV2
MACOS · WINDOWS · LINUX
MASKINHALLEN

01 Install

Mac: unzip, open Terminal and run bash installera.command from the folder. Windows: copy DOSERING.vst3 to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3. Linux: run installera-linux.sh. Restart your DAW. DOSERING is listed among the effects.

Put it on the bass, on a rumble, on a pad, or on the whole mix.

02 The knob

Turn the big knob up and three things happen in order:

  1. DUCKAR / DUCKS from the start: the sound dips in time and comes back, like a sidechain.
  2. FYLLER / FILLS from about 20: a clean sub starts in phase when the kick lets go and is cut when the next kick comes.
  3. RIVER / TEARS from about 45: the low end is saturated. The bass is kept in mono.

The lamps under the knob light up when each stage is working. The thin turquoise arc inside the ring shows how deep the sound is ducked right now. At 0 the sound passes through untouched. Double-click the knob for 50.

03 Modes

1/4 pumps on every quarter note, 1/8 on every eighth, OFFBEAT between the beats. These three follow the DAW tempo and only run while the DAW is playing. LYSSNA / LISTEN listens for the kick in the sound itself and pumps on that, so it needs no tempo. Use it when the kick is part of what you send through DOSERING.

04 The small controls

MIX blends in the untouched sound. UT / OUT is the output level. PUMPA ALLT / PUMP ALL makes the whole sound duck; with it off only the low end ducks and the top stays. SLUMPA / DICE gives a new character in the current mode and leaves the knob where it is.

05 Presets

60 presets, 15 per mode. Step with the arrows at the bottom or click the name for the full list. A preset sets the knob, the mode, the mix and the hidden character: how deep it ducks, the length and bend of the curve, the sub's pitch and decay, how much the low end is torn, and the punch and tail of the sound.

The character values are also available as automation parameters in your DAW, named KARAKTÄR.

06 Good to know

The sub follows the sound: when the input goes silent, as in a breakdown, the sub goes silent too. SV | EN switches language for all MASKINHALLEN machines at once.
